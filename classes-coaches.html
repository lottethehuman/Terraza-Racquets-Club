Policies · 條款

Attendance, Cancellation & Weather

Applies to all 4-week group packages.

適用於所有四週小組課程。

  • Fixed package enrollment locks your 4 weekly slots — individual non-attendance is non-refundable to guarantee court allocation and coach commitment.固定課程報名將鎖定四個每週時段,個別缺席恕不退款,以保障場地及教練安排。
  • Advance absence buffer (24+ hours notice): receive 1 free sister-cohort swap pass to join a parallel cohort in the same month (subject to capacity), or a 100% TRC e-wallet credit refund if no swap is available.提前24小時或以上通知缺席:可獲一次免費調班機會,轉往同月份的平行班別(視乎名額),如無法調班則獲全額TRC電子錢包積分退款。
  • Sick leave policy (under 24 hours notice) requires a HK registered doctor's certificate for 1 class credit extension toward next month's package or court rental (max 1 extension per package).少於24小時通知的病假,須提供香港註冊醫生證明,方可獲一堂課堂積分延期至下月課程或場地租用(每期課程最多延期一次)。
  • Indoor weekday sessions are 100% weather-proof, rain-or-shine, in air-conditioned courts.室內平日班節設有空調,不論天氣,風雨不改。
  • Outdoor weekend sessions are evaluated 2 hours prior — if Amber/Red/Black Rain, Typhoon Signal No. 8+, or a Very Hot Weather Warning (above 33°C) is active, the session moves to that week's indoor weekday slot, or the cohort extends by 1 week if no indoor slot is available.戶外週末班節將於開課前兩小時評估天氣,如發出黃/紅/黑色暴雨警告、八號或以上颱風信號,或酷熱天氣警告(高於33°C),班節將改至該週室內平日時段,若未能安排則整期課程順延一週。

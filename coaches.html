Policies · 條款

Attendance, Cancellation & Weather

  • Individual non-attendance or no-show is non-refundable — the full session fee is forfeited to guarantee court allocation and coach costs.個別缺席或爽約恕不退款,全額課堂費用將被扣取,以保障場地及教練安排。
  • Rescheduling requires 48+ hours' notice.更改時段須提前48小時或以上通知。
  • Weather is evaluated 2 hours before your scheduled session (e.g. a 9:00 AM class is assessed at 7:00 AM).天氣狀況將於課堂開始前兩小時評估(例如上午9時的課堂將於上午7時評估)。
  • If an Amber, Red, or Black Rainstorm Signal, Typhoon Signal No. 8+, or a Very Hot Weather Warning (above 33°C) is in effect, the session is rescheduled and a credit issued.如發出黃/紅/黑色暴雨警告、八號或以上颱風信號,或酷熱天氣警告(高於33°C),課堂將重新安排並發放課堂積分。

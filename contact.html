How To Find Us

By MTR · 港鐵

Take the Tung Chung Line to Olympic Station. Exit A2. Enter Olympian City 1. Turn right, then take the escalator or lift to 2/F.

乘搭東涌線至奧運站,A2出口,進入奧海城一期,右轉後乘扶手電梯或升降機至2樓。

By Car · 自駕

Park in the Olympian City 1 carpark, entering via Hoi Fai Road. Access the mall levels, then take the escalator or lift to 2/F.

可停泊於奧海城一期停車場,經海輝道進入,再乘扶手電梯或升降機至2樓。
